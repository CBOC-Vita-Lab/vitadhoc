# Credits

VitAdhoc was built by AI agents (Claude), and directed and play-tested by a
human (CBOC). ChatGPT helped with package and privacy checks, the licence inventory and
documentation editing.

It stands on the work of many people:

- [**GBAdhoc by ShoshinFauteux**](https://github.com/ShoshinFauteux/GBAdhoc):
  the PSP emulator VitAdhoc grew from, with its link play and frontend.
  Version 1.3 also takes in fixes from its 4.x releases (see the 1.3 notes).
- **gpSP by Exophase**, with the libretro contributors: the GBA emulator and
  its ARM recompiler.
- **TGB Dual:** the Game Boy and Game Boy Color emulator.
- [**Snes9x**](https://github.com/snes9xgit/snes9x) by its many authors: the
  Super NES emulator inside the SNES player (version 1.63), including Shay
  Green's snes_spc sound code.
- [**Genesis Plus GX**](https://github.com/ekeeke/Genesis-Plus-GX) by Charles
  MacDonald and Eke-Eke, with the authors of the cores it includes (Musashi,
  the MAME sound and Z80 cores, Nuked OPN2 / OPLL, blip_buf): the Genesis /
  Mega Drive, Master System, Game Gear and SG-1000 emulator inside the Sega
  player.
- **FCEUmm** by its many authors: the NES emulator. **Beetle WonderSwan**
  (Mednafen's Cygne core, libretro): the WonderSwan emulator.
- **Normmatt and the VBA/VBA-M team:** the open GBA BIOS from ReGBA.
- [**rcheevos**](https://github.com/RetroAchievements/rcheevos) by
  RetroAchievements: the achievements runtime.
- [**Mbed TLS**](https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls) by its contributors (Arm
  Limited and others): the TLS library of online play. **Cloudflare:** the free
  STUN, relay (Realtime TURN) and Workers services online play runs on.
- **mGBA and VisualBoyAdvance-M** authors: the cheat engine and its reference.
- **xerpi and the libvita2d contributors:** graphics. **Sean Barrett and
  contributors:** stb. **Zenju and contributors:** xBRZ. **The Inter and Outfit
  authors:** the fonts.
- **Wikipedia, Wikidata and libretro-database contributors:** game
  descriptions, the cartridge catalogue and the cheat database.
