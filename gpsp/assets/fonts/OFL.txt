The Vita executable embeds Inter Regular/SemiBold and Outfit Medium/SemiBold
TTFs through vita/video_vita_fonts.S. The legacy PSP font atlas is generated
by tools/bake_font.py. Outfit Bold is used to render the LiveArea wordmark.
Complete Inter and Outfit license notices are in the root licenses/ folder
and included with the binary distribution.
