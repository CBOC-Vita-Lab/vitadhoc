The 2x pixel-art scaler in gpsp/vita/mmpx_vita.c follows their rules
(McGuire and Gagiu, "MMPX Style-Preserving Pixel Art Magnification", Journal
of Computer Graphics Techniques, 2021) in a 16-bit RGB565 form, written from
the reference C port at https://github.com/ITotalJustice/mmpx. The reference
files state the notice above and name the MIT licence; that port carries no
separate licence text, so the standard MIT terms follow.
