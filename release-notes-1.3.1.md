## VitAdhoc 1.3.1: snappier menus and a new look

A small update to 1.3, from your feedback.

- **Switching consoles is instant.** Pressing Triangle through GBA, GB, GBC
  and SNES no longer freezes the screen for a moment each time. Measured on
  a Vita: the longest frame while flicking through them went from 85 ms to
  29 ms.
- **Touch feedback.** An on-screen button lights up while your finger is on
  it and flashes when the tap goes through. Slide off to cancel, as before.
- **New bubble and LiveArea art,** with all four consoles in it.

Everything from 1.3 is included: Super NES, split screen, trading between
versions, box art for every game, your own covers, 2x fast forward and sound
in fast forward. [The 1.3 notes](https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vitadhoc/releases/tag/v1.3).

### Install or update

Install `VitAdhoc.vpk` with VitaShell over the existing bubble (title ID
`GBAD00001`), or use the
[CBOC Vita Installer](https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases/latest).
Games, saves and settings stay in place.

`VitAdhoc-install.zip` contains the same VPK, full source, guides and
checksums. No commercial games or proprietary BIOS are included.
